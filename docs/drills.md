# Drill reference

All Brain Age and Brain Age 2 drills plus new candidates. Names here are reference names only; Noggin drills need original names (see design.md).

- Clinical equivalents are an approximate mapping based on how each drill works, not sources Nintendo published. Treat them as close cousins rather than exact matches.
- Drill details for Brain Age and Brain Age 2 are drawn from their Wikipedia articles (en.wikipedia.org).
- Check = part of the Brain Age Check that produced the daily brain age. Exercise = Training mode. Both = appeared in each.
- New idea rows are suggestions for Noggin. Suggested: Score marks quick, fixed difficulty, language free candidates for the Noggin Score test.
- The task ideas are general research paradigms; published test batteries carry copyrighted materials and names, so build original versions.
- Row colours: blue = Brain Age, orange = Brain Age 2, green = new idea.
